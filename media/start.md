## Atelier

Ask a research question. Atelier:

1. plans a search for PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar,
2. ranks what they return and screens every paper against your question,
3. reads the best papers, and
4. writes a cited summary, with the papers under it, best evidence first.

Ask a follow-up to dig into the papers, or tick papers to ask about those alone. A summary downloads as a PDF, its
tables as CSV, and its references for Zotero, EndNote or Mendeley.
